About

Hi, I'm Conor.

I'm a structured creative. I'm happiest where imagination meets a real problem that needs solving: organizing a messy system, designing a better way to work, or shaping raw ideas into something people can use and feel.

My background doesn't follow a straight line, and I've come to see that as the point. I studied music and theatre at Washington College, and I still create: I produce industrial electronic music under the name HARDWEAR. I've also spent years in hospitality, merchant services, and retail. I now work as a retail merchandising associate covering a territory of roughly 570 stores across Maryland.

That field work taught me how much good systems matter. Planning visits across hundreds of locations isn't glamorous, but it's a real design problem, so I treated it as one. I built StoreTracker, my own app for tracking store visits, and I've spent a lot of time working out how to route and sequence a territory more efficiently. Nobody asked me to do either. I did them because I saw a better way and wanted to build it.

That's the pattern in everything I do. I notice things other people tend to overlook: how people actually move through a space, what makes a display land, where a process quietly wastes time. Then I try to make it better, whether the medium is a spreadsheet, a café counter, a piece of software, or a song.
