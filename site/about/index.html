About

Hi, I'm Conor.

I like problems that are part creative and part logistics. A messy process to clean up. A route to plan. A display that isn't selling.

I studied music and theatre at Washington College, and I still make music.

After college I spent seven years running the front of house at a bakery café in Chestertown, then managed beverage programs in Baltimore. From there I moved into field sales, selling payment systems to small businesses. Most recently I worked in retail merchandising for Altria, covering about 570 stores across Maryland.

That's where I started building my own tools. Planning a day across hundreds of stores is a scheduling problem, so I treated it like one. I wrote a route model that cut my driving by 54% and built VisitTracker, an app that logged my store visits on its own. Neither was part of the job.

In every job I've paid attention to the small stuff: how people move through a store, which displays get picked up, where time gets wasted. Then I fix what I can.

How I work

I do my best work when the goal is clear and I'm trusted to figure out how to get there. Give me the target and I'll come back with a plan. I keep my manager posted along the way.

I like variety, a new problem every so often, and a team that can laugh a little. And I care about the people right in front of me: the store manager, the person on the next shift, the regular at the counter.
